## How to use this deliverable

1. Open `dist/Software-Testing-Handbook.pdf` and review the chapters sequentially or focus on recent additions (Chapters 1–6 plus the framework chapters under `chapters/chapter07_doubles.tex` and beyond).
2. Keep an eye out for missing explanations, inconsistent terminology, or choppy transitions between sections.
3. Complete the feedback form in [docs/FEEDBACK_FORM.md](FEEDBACK_FORM.md) and add your comments directly in the document or in a separate response.
4. Return the filled feedback (or a summary) along with any annotated PDF pages so the authors can incorporate the notes before the next draft.
