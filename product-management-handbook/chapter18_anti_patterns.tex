\section{Anti-patterns at Scale}
Scaling Jira without discipline leads to bureaucracy. Avoid the patterns that create noise without decisions.

\subsection{Anti-patterns and fixes}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Status by comment.} Fix: require status updates in fields, not in comments.
  \item \textbf{Field explosion.} Fix: every new field needs an owner and a usage rule.
  \item \textbf{Boards per person.} Fix: build boards per team or workflow, not per individual.
  \item \textbf{Everything is urgent.} Fix: define classes of service and enforce WIP limits.
  \item \textbf{Governance theatre.} Fix: meetings must produce decisions or escalations.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Case study insert}
When every item in PulseBoard was labeled urgent, the team introduced classes of service and removed personal boards to restore flow discipline.
