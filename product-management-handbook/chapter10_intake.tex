\section{Intake and Triage: From Requests to Opportunities}
Raw requests arrive from sales, support, and leadership. Intake turns noise into structured opportunities. Triage decides what is worth discovery and what is not.

\subsection{Jira setup steps (intake)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Create an intake queue (filter + board) scoped to new requests.
  \item Use a dedicated issue type: Opportunity (preferred) or Epic with label "opportunity".
  \item Add fields: Customer Impact, Reach, Confidence, Risk, OKR Link.
  \item Add a lightweight triage workflow: Intake \textrightarrow{} Triage \textrightarrow{} Discovery \textrightarrow{} Parked \textrightarrow{} Closed.
  \item Create automation: if label = "sales" then notify PM; if Risk = High then notify TPM.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Turning requests into opportunities}
An opportunity is a structured hypothesis about customer value. It captures the problem, expected outcome, and why now. Raw requests should never go straight to delivery without a brief.

\subsection{Worked scenario: support escalation becomes an opportunity}
Support reports churn due to slow onboarding. The PM creates an Opportunity issue, links it to the churn OKR, and sets Customer Impact to High. The PO adds a hypothesis: "reducing setup time from 30 to 10 minutes will reduce churn by 5\%." The item moves to Discovery with a clear owner and scope.

\subsection{Case study insert}
OPP-1 enters Intake from support data and is normalized into an Opportunity brief before it becomes INIT-1. The triage decision is recorded before any delivery work starts.
\subsection{Checklist}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Every request is normalized into an Opportunity or Epic with an opportunity label.
  \item Customer Impact, Reach, and Risk are set before Discovery.
  \item No request jumps directly to Ready without a brief.
  \item Parked items have explicit review dates.
  \item Closed items include a reason and evidence.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Failure modes and fixes}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Intake is a dumping ground. \textbf{Fix:} Enforce triage within 7 days.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Opportunities are vague. \textbf{Fix:} Require a hypothesis and outcome metric.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Everything becomes a top priority. \textbf{Fix:} Use explicit scoring and tie-breakers.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Parked items never return. \textbf{Fix:} Add review dates and automated reminders.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Requests bypass the system. \textbf{Fix:} Block delivery without an Opportunity link.
\end{enumerate}
