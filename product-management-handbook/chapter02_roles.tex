\section{Roles and Responsibilities in Jira}
Jira only works when ownership is explicit. PMs define outcomes and prioritize, POs keep the backlog clean and executable, TPMs manage dependencies and delivery risk, and EMs own execution health. Jira makes these responsibilities visible if each role owns specific artifacts.

\subsection{Roles mapped to Jira artifacts}
\textbf{PM:} owns Initiatives/Epics, outcome metrics, and prioritization fields.\\
\textbf{PO:} owns Story/Task readiness, acceptance criteria, and backlog hygiene.\\
\textbf{TPM:} owns dependencies, delivery risks, release coordination.\\
\textbf{EM:} owns workflow health, WIP, and team commitments.

\subsection{Worked scenario: triaging a cross-team dependency}
A Story is blocked by a data migration owned by another team. The TPM creates a dependency link and adds a Risk field update. The EM sets a WIP limit to prevent new work while blocked. The PM adjusts the Epic timeline and logs the impact on the Initiative. Jira now shows both the dependency and the business impact.

\subsection{Case study insert}
For OPP-2 and EPIC-5, the PM owns outcome fields, the PO keeps ST-115 and ST-116 in Ready, the TPM manages the dependency on TS-117, and the EM tracks WIP on the Monetization board.
\subsection{Checklist}
\begin{itemize}
  \item PMs own outcome fields and Epic priority ordering.
  \item POs keep Stories in Ready with clear acceptance criteria.
  \item TPMs update dependency links and delivery risks weekly.
  \item EMs enforce WIP limits and update sprint commitments.
  \item Each artifact has a single accountable owner.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Failure modes and fixes}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Failure:} No one owns dependency links. \textbf{Fix:} Assign TPM ownership and review weekly.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} PMs adjust scope but Jira stays stale. \textbf{Fix:} Require Epic updates before roadmap reviews.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} POs accept Stories without criteria. \textbf{Fix:} Gate Ready status on acceptance fields.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} EMs use Jira only after slips. \textbf{Fix:} Review WIP and flow weekly.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Multiple owners update the same field. \textbf{Fix:} Define field ownership and enforce it.
\end{enumerate}
