\section{High-Leverage Habits: Triage, Refinement, Review Cadence}
Jira only works if the team runs the system. High-leverage habits keep the backlog clean, the flow predictable, and the reporting credible. The goal is to make decisions quickly and consistently.

\subsection{Cadences that keep the system healthy}
Triage happens daily or twice weekly to prevent backlog rot. Refinement happens weekly to keep Ready items small and clear. Reviews happen on sprint or release cadence to keep commitments real. Each cadence updates fields that drive decisions: priority, risk, dependency, and target release.

\subsection{Worked scenario: weekly refinement}
The PO reviews the backlog and moves two items to Ready after adding acceptance criteria. The PM adjusts priority based on customer impact. The TPM flags a dependency and adds a mitigation note. The EM confirms capacity and pulls the top item into the sprint. Jira now reflects a credible plan.

\subsection{Case study insert}
During refinement, ST-102 and ST-116 move to Ready once acceptance criteria and Risk are filled. The PM reprioritizes EPIC-2 based on BG-122 frequency, and the update is visible in Jira.
\subsection{Checklist}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Daily or twice-weekly triage clears new intake.
  \item Weekly refinement ensures Ready items meet DoR.
  \item Sprint/release reviews update Target Release and Risk.
  \item Backlog size stays within a 1--2 sprint horizon.
  \item Priority changes are logged and owned.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Failure modes and fixes}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Backlog grows without pruning. \textbf{Fix:} Archive or close items that miss the next two cycles.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Refinement skips acceptance criteria. \textbf{Fix:} Enforce DoR before moving to Ready.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Triage becomes a status meeting. \textbf{Fix:} Limit triage to intake decisions only.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Reviews focus on activity, not outcomes. \textbf{Fix:} Tie review agenda to Epics and outcomes.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Priority changes are ad hoc. \textbf{Fix:} Require explicit customer impact and risk updates.
\end{enumerate}
