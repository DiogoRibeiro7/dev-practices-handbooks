\section{Discovery in Jira: Hypotheses, Assumptions, Experiments}
Discovery turns opportunities into decisions. Jira can track the evidence without bloating the delivery backlog if you keep discovery issues lightweight and time-boxed.

\subsection{Jira setup steps (discovery)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Add an Opportunity issue type or use Epics as opportunities.
  \item Create a Discovery workflow: Intake \textrightarrow{} Discovery \textrightarrow{} Validated \textrightarrow{} Planned \textrightarrow{} Done.
  \item Create an Experiment issue type (or Task subtype) linked to the Opportunity.
  \item Add fields: Hypothesis, Assumptions, Evidence, Confidence.
  \item Create a board or filter for Discovery items only.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Hypotheses and assumptions}
Hypotheses describe expected outcomes. Assumptions describe what must be true for the outcome to hold. Keep both short and testable. Store them directly on the Opportunity or Experiment issue.

\subsection{Experiment tasks}
Each experiment is a time-boxed task with a clear success signal. Experiments are not features. They exist to reduce uncertainty.

\subsection{Worked scenario: pricing hypothesis}
The PM creates an Opportunity: "increase conversion on the Pro plan." Assumptions include "teams will pay for audit trails." An Experiment task is created to run a pricing test for two weeks. The outcome is recorded in Evidence and Confidence is updated before moving to Validated.

\subsection{Case study insert}
EXP-1 tests a shorter setup wizard for OPP-1. Evidence is captured in the Opportunity so INIT-1 can move to Validated.
\subsection{Checklist}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Every Opportunity has a hypothesis and success metric.
  \item Assumptions are explicit and limited to 3--5 items.
  \item Experiments are time-boxed and linked to the Opportunity.
  \item Confidence is updated after evidence is collected.
  \item Validated means evidence exists, not just stakeholder agreement.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Failure modes and fixes}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Discovery tasks linger indefinitely. \textbf{Fix:} Time-box experiments and close stale ones.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Hypotheses are too vague. \textbf{Fix:} Require a measurable outcome.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Evidence is scattered in docs. \textbf{Fix:} Store the summary in Jira fields.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Experiments become delivery work. \textbf{Fix:} Enforce size limits and separate issue type.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Confidence never changes. \textbf{Fix:} Update Confidence as a step in Discovery review.
\end{enumerate}
