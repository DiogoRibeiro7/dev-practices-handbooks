\section{Prioritization in Jira: RICE, WSJF, and Tie-Breakers}
Prioritization frameworks keep debate focused on evidence. Jira can encode RICE or WSJF using simple fields so scoring is transparent and repeatable.

\subsection{Jira setup steps (prioritization)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Add fields: Reach (number), Impact (single select), Confidence (single select), Effort (number).
  \item Create a custom field for RICE Score (number) or calculate externally and paste.
  \item Optional: add WSJF fields (Cost of Delay, Job Size).
  \item Build a dashboard gadget that sorts by score.
  \item Define tie-breaker rules in the project README or Confluence page.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{RICE implementation}
\textbf{RICE} = $(Reach \times Impact \times Confidence) / Effort$. Use a fixed scale for Impact and Confidence (e.g., 0.5, 1, 2). Keep scales consistent across teams.

\subsection{WSJF implementation (optional)}
WSJF = Cost of Delay / Job Size. Use it for portfolio-level trade-offs or large bets where delay cost is measurable.

\subsection{Tie-breakers when scores are close}
When scores are within 10\%, use a tie-breaker list: risk reduction first, dependency unblock second, then time sensitivity. Document the decision in the Opportunity or Epic.

\subsection{Worked scenario: two similar opportunities}
Two Opportunities score 18 and 17 on RICE. The higher-risk item unlocks a partner integration. The PM applies the tie-breaker: dependency unblock wins. The decision is recorded and visible.

\subsection{Case study insert}
OPP-2 scores slightly lower than OPP-1 on RICE, but the tie-breaker favors dependency unblock on EPIC-5 because TS-117 is on the critical path.
\subsection{Checklist}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Scoring fields are present and required before Ready.
  \item Impact and Confidence use a shared scale.
  \item Scores are recalculated when assumptions change.
  \item Tie-breaker rules are documented and used.
  \item Decisions are recorded on the issue.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Failure modes and fixes}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Scores are inflated to win priority. \textbf{Fix:} Require evidence links for high scores.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Multiple scoring systems run in parallel. \textbf{Fix:} Pick one per product area.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Teams ignore tie-breakers. \textbf{Fix:} Make tie-breakers part of review cadence.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Effort is missing or inconsistent. \textbf{Fix:} Standardize estimation units.
  \item \textbf{Failure:} Scores never change post-discovery. \textbf{Fix:} Re-score after new evidence.
\end{enumerate}
