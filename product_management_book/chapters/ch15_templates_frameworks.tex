\chapter{Templates and Frameworks for Different Work Types}
\label{ch:templates-frameworks}

Templates convert patterns into actionable checklists. Teams using structured templates report fewer clarification loops; Jira and GitHub cite double-digit drops in back-and-forth after rolling out required issue forms. This chapter presents core templates for common work types and provides guidance on adapting them to your tooling.

\section{Template Design Principles}
\label{sec:template-principles}

Good templates strike a balance between structure and flexibility.

\subsection{Completeness vs. Simplicity}
Include sections that drive quality but avoid overwhelming authors. Use required and optional sections, plus progressive disclosure (collapsible details or checklists) to keep forms approachable.

\subsection{Clarity and Examples}
Provide instructions next to each section, along with good/bad examples. Inline tooltips or hyperlinks to exemplar tickets accelerate adoption.

\subsection{Tool Integration}
Templates must fit within Jira, GitHub Issues, or other tools. Use Markdown headings, tables, and links to keep content readable. Where tools support custom fields, map template sections to structured inputs for reporting.

\subsection{Adaptability}
Version templates and allow teams to fork them. Collect feedback regularly and document why sections exist so future editors avoid accidental regressions.

\section{Feature Development Template}
\label{sec:feature-template}

For user-facing features, emphasize user value, impact, and technical considerations.

\subsection{Template Structure}
Sections include: problem statement, personas, user journeys, success metrics, acceptance criteria, non-functional requirements, dependencies, risks, and rollout plan. Embed links to design assets and instrumentation specs.

\section{Data Exploration Template}
\label{sec:exploration-template}

Exploratory work requires structure without stifling discovery.

\subsection{Template Structure}
Capture the questions to answer, datasets, access requirements, analysis methods, time box, deliverables (findings memo, dashboard), and decision criteria.

\subsection{Sample Header}
Analysts often start with a lightweight Markdown block copied into an analytics ticket or shared doc. Keep the header constrained to the most important metadata and link deeper artifacts elsewhere.

\begin{verbatim}
## Exploration Title
Customer Churn Cohort Deep-Dive

## Question(s)
- Which cohorts are driving churn >8%?
- What leading indicators predict churn 2 weeks out?

## Datasets / Access
- warehouse.mart.churn_events (owner: data_eng)
- s3://telemetry/ios (request access via #data-ops)

## Time Box
Start: 2025-11-20  End: 2025-12-05 (10 working days)

## Deliverables
- Findings memo + one-pager
- Mode dashboard link + SQL in repo analytics/churn/
\end{verbatim}

\subsection{Complete Example: Customer Churn Analysis}
Demonstrate how a churn exploration defines cohorts, details data access steps, lists analyses (cohort retention, feature importance), and triggers a go/no-go decision for model development.

\subsection{Variations}
Offer short-form templates for quick assessments and long-form for multi-week studies, plus a variant for ongoing monitoring reports.

\section{Automated Template Generators}
\label{sec:template-generators}

Scaling templates across teams benefits from automation. The repository now includes \texttt{tools/template\_generator.py}, a CLI utility that:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Accepts work type, domain, and risk level, then stitches the appropriate section list with domain/risk-specific extensions.
    \item Converts free-form requirement statements into structured acceptance criteria using lightweight NLP rules.
    \item Validates completeness by running predefined checklists aligned with the frameworks in this chapter.
    \item Exports the assembled template to Markdown, Jira JSON payloads, and Azure DevOps YAML for rapid ingestion into planning tools.
\end{itemize}

\begin{lstlisting}[language=bash,caption={Generating a healthcare feature template with high-risk controls.}]
python tools/template_generator.py \
  --work-type feature \
  --domain healthcare \
  --risk high \
  --requirement "Improve clinician onboarding to cut activation time by 15%" \
  --export markdown jira azure
\end{lstlisting}

Outputs land in \texttt{tools/generated/}, ready to copy into tickets or share with stakeholders. Detailed instructions live in \texttt{tools/README.md}. Teams can wire the generator into internal portals or notebook workflows to offer self-service templates with consistent structure, automated acceptance criteria, and built-in validation.

\section{Template Customization Guide}
\label{sec:customization}

Tailor templates without losing their benefits.

\subsection{Assessing Your Needs}
Determine which problems you are solving and gather feedback from consumers of the template (engineers, analysts, stakeholders).

\subsection{Modification Process}
Start from the closest template, adjust sections, pilot with a small team, and iterate. Document changes and rationale.

\subsection{Creating New Templates}
When patterns repeat but lack templates, generalize them. Capture two or three examples before codifying to avoid premature standardization.

\subsection{Template Governance}
Assign owners, version templates, and publish change logs. Provide training when updates introduce new sections.

\label{sec:tool-adaptations}

\section{Summary}
\label{sec:templates-summary}

Templates codify best practices for repeated work types. By designing them thoughtfully, integrating them with tooling, and continuously iterating, organizations create a scalable foundation for high-quality requirements. The next chapter focuses on metrics-driven acceptance patterns that complement these templates.

\section{Industry Adaptations}
\label{sec:templates-industry}
\paragraph{Regulated Industries.} Add fields for regulatory references, approval IDs, and validation evidence. Include checklists for privacy/security reviews and attach links to controlled documents for auditors.

\paragraph{Startups vs. Enterprises.} Startups should keep templates lightweight and embed them directly in tools (Notion, Jira) to avoid context switching. Enterprises can leverage advanced automation (workflow approvals, reporting) and align templates with portfolio governance.

\paragraph{B2B vs. B2C.} B2B templates should capture account-specific requirements, success criteria tied to contracts, and go-live enablement tasks for customer success. B2C templates need fields for segmentation, marketing plans, and experiment IDs.

\paragraph{Hardware-Software Integration.} Include fields for hardware version, BOM updates, manufacturing checkpoints, and lab test requirements. Ensure templates track both firmware/software dependencies and physical QA sign-offs.

\subsection{Action Checklist}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Pick one work type (feature, platform, research) and customize the template fields directly in your tracking tool.
    \item Add inline guidance or examples for fields that routinely confuse authors.
    \item Set a quarterly reminder to review template usage metrics and retire unused sections.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Try this now (5 min).} Open your current ticket template and add a short placeholder for ``Success Metric: baseline $\rightarrow$ target.'' Save it and notify the team.

\section{Warning Signs and Recovery}
\label{sec:templates-warning}
\begin{description}
    \item[\textbf{Warning sign}] Teams treat templates as optional or strip them down until only titles remain.
    \item[\textbf{Recovery}] Make key fields mandatory in tooling and explain the business impact of each; rotate template champions to gather feedback.
    \item[\textbf{Warning sign}] Templates become bloated checklists, slowing authors and encouraging copy-paste.
    \item[\textbf{Recovery}] Review usage metrics quarterly, remove unused sections, and provide examples that set expectations for brevity vs. detail.
\end{description}

\section{Triple-Lens Takeaways}
\label{sec:templates-triple}

\begin{description}
    \item[\textbf{Busy PM}] Bookmark the feature, platform, and research templates—drop them directly into Jira or GitHub so you can move faster without sacrificing clarity.
    \item[\textbf{Technical Lead}] Customize the automation hooks and tool-integration guidance so that template sections map to structured fields, enabling reporting and guardrail enforcement.
    \item[\textbf{Executive}] Review template adoption metrics as a leading indicator of operational maturity; when teams use common forms, portfolio-level comparisons become easier and risk surfaces sooner.
\end{description}

\section{Implementation Snapshot}
\label{sec:templates-snapshot}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Time investment}—1 sprint to tailor templates, configure tooling, and coach initial users.
    \item \textbf{ROI timeline}—Clarification requests drop almost immediately; template analytics improve over the next month.
    \item \textbf{Risk mitigation}—Templates ensure critical fields (metrics, risks, dependencies) are captured before work starts.
    \item \textbf{Competitive advantage}—Structured inputs enable better portfolio reporting and faster decision-making than ad-hoc issue descriptions.
    \item \textbf{Cost of inaction}—Teams keep reinventing formats, causing inconsistent data, manual reporting, and uneven execution quality.
\end{itemize}
