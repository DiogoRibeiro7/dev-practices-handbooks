\chapter{Architecture choices}

There is no perfect architecture.
You choose based on team size, domain complexity, and operational maturity.

\section{Monoliths}
Monoliths are simpler to build and deploy.
They are often the right choice early on.

\section{Microservices}
Microservices introduce operational and coordination costs.
Only split when you need independent scaling or ownership.

\section{Modular monoliths}
A modular monolith keeps one deployable but enforces internal boundaries.
It is often the best of both worlds.

\section{Decision criteria}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item team ownership boundaries
  \item scaling needs
  \item release cadence
  \item dependency isolation
\end{itemize}

\begin{checklistbox}{Checklist: architecture}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Prefer a monolith until you cannot.
  \item Split services only with clear ownership.
  \item Keep boundaries explicit.
\end{itemize}
\end{checklistbox}
