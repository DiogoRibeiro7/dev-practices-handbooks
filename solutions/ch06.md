# Chapter 06 Solutions

> ⚠️ **Spoiler alert:** These notes resolve the Chapter 06 exercises.

1. Running `python examples/ch06/capstone_readiness.py` prints each pillar; list each item in your notes (risk_score, pyramid_balance, automation_status, kpi_narrative, governance sign-off).
2. The `examples/ch06/solutions/extended_readiness.py` script adds a pyramid coverage badge and doughnut mention to the output; run it to confirm the extra fields appear.
3. A readiness narrative could be “KPI burn dropped 4% under budget because the regression suite stayed healthy, so we proceed with the release.” Capture supporting evidence in your doc.
4. Model governance failure by toggling `governance_signoff = None` and documenting the escalation (notify QA lead, pause merge, assign follow-up).
5. Share the log with a teammate, debate the missing artifacts, and record the consensus remediation steps.
6. Checklist template can be a table capturing pillars and yes/no columns; reuse it per release to confirm each pillar is satisfied.
7. Measure gate-to-readiness time (e.g., 2 min) and note steps to reduce it (parallel report generation, cached dashboards).
8. Collaborate with your PM to craft a slide that lists each readiness pillar, its status, and the accompanying narrative; keep it simple for demos.
